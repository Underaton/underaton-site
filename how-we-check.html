How we check deals: our editorial code

Our editorial code · last updated 19 November 2026

Short version: we list a deal only when the retailer's own website shows a genuine reduction on a price it actually charged. We re-check every deal at least once a day and remove it as soon as it ends, sells out or goes back up. Brands can't pay to be listed.

How we check a deal

  1. On the retailer's own website. We check the price on the retailer's own product page (occasionally its listing page, which we say in the deal's note). We don't use prices from other deals sites or forums.
  2. A genuine "was" price. The higher price must be one the retailer itself shows it charged. We don't use RRPs the item was never sold at, or "worth" values on gift sets.
  3. Every morning, and again at midday in peak weeks. If the price has gone up, the item has sold out or the offer has ended, the deal comes off the site that day. If we can't re-check it, we remove it rather than leave an old price up.
  4. Sold by the retailer. We only list items sold by the retailer itself, not by marketplace sellers on its site.

What the Real deal check means

Every deal shows a short line from our own records, for example "Checked 9 times since 17 Nov. Same price at every check." It tells you:

  • How many times we've checked it and when we first spotted it.
  • Whether the price has moved: "Dropped from £X" if it got cheaper, "Lowest price we've seen" if today is the lowest of several different prices, or "Same price at every check" if nothing has changed.

It covers only the period since we started tracking an item, so it can't tell you whether the item was cheaper months ago. For big purchases, it's always worth checking a price-history tool and the retailer's price-match policy too.

Our rules

  • Brands can't pay to appear. Which deals we list, and in what order, is decided only by the saving and how useful we think it is.
  • Featured partners are labelled. Our homepage has a clearly marked "Featured partner" strip for retailers we have an affiliate partnership with. Those deals pass exactly the same price checks, and the main list is still ordered by saving.
  • A good deal is listed whether or not it pays us. Many of our links earn nothing. Affiliate links are marked with an asterisk (*) on the button.
  • No unauthorised voucher codes, and no fake urgency: an "Ends" time only appears when the retailer states one.
  • Every social post is labelled #ad.
  • Mistakes get fixed fast. Spot a wrong price? Email hello@underaton.co.uk and we'll correct or remove it the same day.
