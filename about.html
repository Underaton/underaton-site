About Under a Ton

Making great value easy to find, for shoppers and brands alike

Under a Ton exists to make good value easy to find. Every day, UK retailers run thousands of offers, and shoppers have to wade through them to find a gift that's genuinely worth buying. We do that work: we find the best real reductions under £100 (in British slang, "a ton" is £100), check them, and organise them the way people actually shop.

What we believe

  • A deal should be real. We only list a reduction when the retailer itself shows a higher price it has charged, and we never count "worth" values on gift sets.
  • Shopping should start with the person, not the product. So we sort deals by budget (under £20, £50 and £100), by who they're for and by occasion, from Secret Santa to Valentine's Day.
  • Trust is the whole business. Every price is checked on the retailer's own website each morning, every page shows when it was checked, and every commercial link is labelled.

What we do for shoppers

  • Save time: the day's best gifts and deals in one place, already filtered by budget, recipient and occasion.
  • Save money with confidence: only genuine reductions, checked daily, with expired or sold-out deals removed.
  • Buy direct: you always buy from the retailer itself, with its own delivery, returns and customer service. We never sell anything or ask for your payment details.
  • Get it delivered to your phone: the 10 best deals each morning on our social channels.

What we do for brands and retailers

  • Reach shoppers who are ready to buy: people come to Under a Ton with a budget, a person in mind and an occasion, often with a deadline like Christmas.
  • Get presented accurately: correct prices, real reductions, retailer names used only to say where a deal is available, and no unauthorised voucher codes or brand-term bidding.
  • Get seen in more places: featured deals appear on our website, in our gift guides and in daily short-form video and social posts.

If you're a brand or retailer, see Work with us.

How everyone wins

Shoppers find better gifts for less, and come back because they can trust what they see. Retailers sell more to people who arrive ready to buy, without diluting their brand. And because we're paid a small commission by retailers rather than by shoppers, our incentive is simple: help people find something they're genuinely happy to buy.

How we work

Under a Ton is an independent UK business. We use technology, including AI tools, to search retailers and check prices every morning, but the rules about what gets listed are ours and are applied to every deal. Read our affiliate disclosure to see how we're funded.
