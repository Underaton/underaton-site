Privacy & cookies

Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data is collected when you use underaton.co.uk, and your rights under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

Under a Ton (underaton.co.uk) is an independent UK business and is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: hello@underaton.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we keep your email address and message so we can reply. We keep this correspondence for up to 2 years, then delete it.
  • We don't run our own tracking or analytics cookies, ask you to create an account, or take payments on this site.

Services that see technical data

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.
  • Fonts: our typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which see your IP address.

Affiliate links and retailer cookies

When you click a deal, you leave our site for the retailer's website. If the link is an affiliate link, the affiliate network (for example Awin, CJ or Impact) and the retailer may set cookies on your device so they can tell that your purchase came from Under a Ton. Those cookies are controlled by the network and the retailer, not by us, and their own privacy and cookie policies apply. We don't receive your name, address or payment details, only anonymous sales totals.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@underaton.co.uk. We'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we add analytics, a newsletter or anything else that collects personal data, we'll update this page first, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
