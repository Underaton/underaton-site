What is Under a Ton?

Under a Ton is a UK deals site that lists the best genuine reductions under £100 from big UK retailers such as M&S, Currys, Argos, John Lewis and Boots, sorted into deals under £20, £50 and £100, by who they're for and by occasion. Every price is checked on the retailer's own website every morning.

Are the discounts genuine?

We only list an item when the retailer's own website shows a higher price it actually charged before. We don't use 'worth' values on gift sets, and we don't publish voucher codes we aren't authorised to share.

Where do I buy the deals?

Directly from the retailer. Every link takes you to the retailer's website, so their prices, delivery, returns and Christmas returns policies apply.
