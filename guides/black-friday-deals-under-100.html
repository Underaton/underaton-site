Buying tips

  • Check the price at checkout: Black Friday prices can change several times a day.
  • Look at the delivery date if it's a Christmas present. Most retailers publish last order dates in early December.
  • Click and collect is often free at Argos, John Lewis and M&S, and can save on delivery costs.

When is Black Friday 2026 in the UK?

Black Friday 2026 is Friday 27 November, and Cyber Monday is Monday 30 November. Many UK retailers start their Black Friday sales a week or more before.

Is it worth waiting until Black Friday itself?

Not always. Many retailers cut prices early and some items sell out before the day. If something is already at a price you're happy with, it can be sensible to buy it and check the retailer's price-match or returns policy.
