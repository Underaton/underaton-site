Black Friday 2026 UK: dates, deals and how to spot a fake discount

Last checked 21 November 2026

Quick answerBlack Friday 2026 is on Friday 27 November in the UK, and Cyber Monday is Monday 30 November. That's 6 days away, but most big UK retailers have already started cutting prices. How to spot a fake Black Friday discount

  • Check the "was" price. A real reduction is against a price the retailer actually charged. Be wary of "RRP" or "worth" values that the item was never sold at.
  • Compare the model number. Electricals sometimes have a Black-Friday-only model with a slightly different spec. Search the exact code.
  • Look at the price history. If the same item was cheaper in the summer sale, the "deal" may not be the best price of the year.
  • Check who sells it. Marketplace sellers on big retail sites have their own returns policies. We only list items sold by the retailer itself.
  • Read the returns window. Many retailers extend Christmas returns into January. Check the retailer's own page before you buy.

Which UK retailers take part?

Almost all the big UK retailers run Black Friday offers, including M&S, Currys, Argos, John Lewis, Boots, Next, Very, Dunelm, AO, JD Sports and Foot Locker. We list the best genuine reductions under £100 from each one on our retailer pages, checked every morning.

Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday 27 November 2026. Cyber Monday follows on Monday 30 November 2026.

When do Black Friday sales start in the UK?

Many UK retailers start a week or more before the day itself, and some run offers through the whole of November. Prices can change daily, so we re-check every deal each morning.

Is Black Friday really cheaper?

Sometimes. Many reductions are genuine, but some 'was' prices are inflated. Only trust a discount when the retailer actually charged the higher price before; that's the rule we use for every deal we list.

Is Cyber Monday better than Black Friday?

Usually the offers are similar, and popular items can sell out over the weekend. If the price is already good, waiting rarely gets you a much better one.

Can I return Black Friday purchases bought as Christmas gifts?

Most big UK retailers extend their returns window over Christmas, often into January. Check the retailer's own returns page before you buy, as policies differ.
