Tips to beat the Christmas cut-off

  • Order large items (furniture, appliances) earliest: they usually have the first cut-off.
  • Click & collect often runs later than home delivery and is frequently free.
  • If you miss the date, a gift card or e-voucher can be sent instantly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last day to post for Christmas 2026?

Royal Mail's recommended last posting dates for UK inland mail are Royal Mail 2nd Class: Friday 18 December; Royal Mail Tracked 48: Saturday 19 December; Royal Mail 1st Class: Monday 21 December; Royal Mail Tracked 24: Monday 21 December; Royal Mail Special Delivery Guaranteed: Wednesday 23 December.

When do retailers publish their Christmas delivery dates?

Most big UK retailers publish them in late November or early December. We check each retailer's own delivery page every morning and update this page.

Is click and collect later than home delivery?

Often, yes. Several big UK retailers have offered click and collect up to 23 or 24 December in past years. Check the retailer's own page for this year's times.

What if I miss the last order date?

Many retailers offer gift cards or e-gift vouchers that can be sent by email instantly, and some offer same-day delivery or collection in selected areas.
