Work with us

For brands, retailers and affiliate programmes

Under a Ton helps UK shoppers find genuine deals and gifts under £100, sorted by budget, recipient and occasion. We work with brands and retailers through affiliate programmes on Awin, CJ, Impact and Partnerize, and welcome direct conversations about featuring your offers.

Who we reach

  • UK shoppers buying gifts and treats, with a clear budget and occasion in mind, especially around Black Friday, Christmas, the January sales, Valentine's Day, and Mother's and Father's Day.
  • People looking for value from trusted, well-known retailers rather than unknown marketplaces.

Where your offers can appear

  • Our website: the daily deals feed, filtered by price, recipient, occasion and category.
  • Gift guides: Secret Santa under £20, gifts for him and her, kids' gifts, beauty and fragrance, tech, and home and kitchen.
  • Retailer pages: a dedicated, daily-updated page of your best offers under £100.
  • Social and video: daily carousels, Stories and short-form video on Instagram, Threads and other channels, all labelled #ad.

Our commitments to you

  • Prices and reductions are checked on your own website every morning and removed when they end.
  • We only show a "was" price that you have genuinely charged.
  • We only use voucher codes and creative supplied through your affiliate programme.
  • We never bid on your brand name in paid search or use your logo to suggest an official partnership.
  • All commercial content is clearly labelled, in line with UK Advertising Standards Authority and CAP Code guidance.
  • Brand-safe presentation: deals are shown alongside other well-known UK retailers, with no adult, gambling or misleading content.

Get in touch

To discuss featuring your products, an exclusive offer for our audience, or our application to your programme, email hello@underaton.co.uk with "Partnership" in the subject line.
